We introduce QuantSightBench, a benchmark for evaluating LLM forecasting through prediction intervals rather than multiple-choice formats. We find that none of the 11 evaluated frontier and open-weight models reach the 90% coverage target, revealing systematic overconfidence, particularly when estimating extreme values.
